About Sugar Saints

Hi, I’m Irene — a busy mom of three who turns sugar and royal icing into edible art. What started as a creative outlet has grown into Sugar Saints Cookie Company, where every cookie is designed and hand-decorated with care.

I specialize in custom cookies for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, sports events, corporate gifts, and any occasion worth celebrating. Based in Brighton, Colorado, I create small-batch orders that feel personal and special.

Each design is made to order. I love bringing your ideas to life — whether it’s a simple monogram or an elaborate themed set.
